Static Structural Analysis of Autonomous Leaf Vacuum Robot Chassis

CADD Centre · Finite Element Analysis
Completed

Executed static structural finite element analysis on a leaf vacuum robot chassis modeled in Siemens NX to evaluate load capacity, deformation, and stress distribution under full operational payloads prior to manufacturing.

  • Modeled a 3D structural assembly chassis in Siemens NX comprising 50×50 mm tubular frames, 3 mm baseplates, and 6 mm wheel brackets.
  • Conducted static FEA in ANSYS applying combined vertical loads (2,550 N total across hopper, blower, and electronics) under standard earth gravity and 8 fixed support points.
  • Evaluated stress distribution and deformation, achieving a peak von Mises stress of 121.9 MPa and a maximum deflection of 2.38 mm.
  • Validated frame integrity with a Minimum Factor of Safety of 2.05 against structural steel yield strength (250 MPa), certifying the design for fabrication.
Software :Siemens NX, ANSYS 2020 R1
Scope :Static Structural Analysis

Industrial Shredder Shaft — Reverse Engineering & Reassembly

Conestoga College · Multi-format CAD recovery
Case Study

A recycling facility's industrial shredder suffered structural failure after hopper overload bent the hex shaft and damaged multiple cutting blades. With the OEM out of business, the task was to reconstruct the full shaft assembly from a fragmented, multi-vendor CAD dataset into one clean, fully-defined SolidWorks model.

  • Imported and repaired non-native geometry (Inventor .ipt, STEP, Parasolid) using SolidWorks feature recognition, rebuilding editable feature trees from dumb solids
  • Fully defined every sketch with dimensional constraints — no floating geometry
  • Assembled the complete shaft stack: hex shaft, spur gear, blades, spacers, and divertors, with correct mating and rotational orientation
  • Structured part properties (material, finish, drafter, date) and renamed features to reflect actual part geometry for downstream drafting
Software :SolidWorks
Scope :CAD Assembly
